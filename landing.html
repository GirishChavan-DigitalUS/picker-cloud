Capabilities

Everything you need
to read the tape

A complete intraday toolkit — from raw price data to AI-generated trade signals.

📡

Real-Time WebSocket Feed

Prices, indicators, and AI signals stream via a persistent WebSocket with auto-reconnect and timeframe-aware routing. Zero page refreshes needed.

🤖

AI UP / DOWN Predictions

33-rule weighted evaluator scores EMA, VWAP, RSI, volume, candle structure, ORB, S/R, and multi-indicator confluence signals. Returns a directional prediction with confidence score, per-rule explanations, and optional LLM plain-English commentary.

📊

Candlestick Chart + Overlays

Lightweight Charts v5 with session background shading (pre-market, regular, after-hours), ORB, VWAP, PM/PD price lines, and swing markers.

📐

20+ Technical Indicators

EMA 9/21/50 with spread %, RSI-14 (Wilder), RVOL / volume state, VWAP position & motion, daily trend, POC, ORB 15m, premarket H/L, previous day H/L, support & resistance, swing H/L, 9-signal confluence score, plus candle structure, pattern detection, and volume character from the candle context engine.

📋

12-Ticker Watchlist

SPY, QQQ, SPX, AAPL, GOOGL, NVDA, TSLA, AMZN, MSFT, PLTR, META, AMD — all scheduled every 60 seconds. Add any ticker temporarily during a session; the list resets on refresh to keep performance predictable.

🚨

Composite Alert System

Five tiered pattern detectors (T1–T3): POWER_TREND, STRUCTURE_BREAK, VWAP_RESET, EXHAUSTION_REVERSAL, and POC_MAGNET. Alerts are batch-displayed per refresh cycle with tier filters and a manual ↻ Refresh button. Deduped within 15 minutes.

🧠

Unified Market Read + Confluence

Market Read now includes the confluence summary inline: BUY/SELL/MIXED bias, decision strength score, and plain-English explanation so non-traders can interpret signal alignment quickly.

🔬

Tape Read + Candle Context

Candle context engine analyses the last 30 minutes of 5-min bars: detects 9 candlestick patterns (Engulfing, Hammer, Doji, InsideBar, NR7, rejection wicks), price structure (UPTREND/DOWNTREND/COILING/CHOPPY), volume character, and key-level proximity. Click Generate for a 3-line LLM senior-trader narrative covering tape environment, dominant setup, and next probable move.

↔

Resizable Panels

Drag the divider between the watchlist and detail view, or between the chart and indicators sidebar, to set your preferred workspace layout.

📲

Progressive Web App (PWA)

Install Picker on any device via Share → Add to Home Screen (iOS) or Chrome's install prompt (Android). Launches in standalone mode — no browser chrome, no URL bar. App shell loads instantly even offline.

🔔

Web Push Notifications

Subscribe once by tapping 🔕 in the Alerts panel. Tier 1 (ACT) and Tier 2 (WATCH) composite alerts are delivered via Web Push (VAPID) — directly to your OS notification tray even when the app is closed or the screen is off. Tap any notification to jump straight to the alert ticker's chart.

🗓

InsightGrid — Pattern Detection

Main dashboard shows a per-ticker insight card for all 12 symbols. Each card runs a live pattern engine (Breakout, Fakeout, Reversal, Rejection, Bounce, Continuation, Buildup, Chop Zone) and displays the current pattern label, signal age (~Nm), HTF daily bias (D↑/D↓), and 10-step history strip. Works on both desktop and mobile.

📊

Macro Index Alignment Banner

A sticky row above the insight cards shows SPY · QQQ · SPX daily trend with an alignment label — Macro Tailwind ↑, Macro Headwind ↓, Lean Bullish, Lean Bearish, or Index Conflict ⚠. Instantly contextualises every individual stock signal against the macro backdrop.

✔

Signal Context Layer

Every card shows volume confirmation (rvol ×, HIGH/LOW/NORMAL), signal age in minutes (green = fresh, red = stale), HTF daily bias, and directional badges (VWAP BUY/SELL and Confluence BUY/SELL/MIXED). A built-in ? Help guide explains each element for new users.

⏱

Multi-Timeframe (2m / 5m / 15m)

Switch the entire dashboard between three intraday timeframes with a single click in the header (2m / 5m / 15m). All candles, indicators, predictions, composite alerts, and the WebSocket stream re-subscribe automatically. Refresh cadence adapts per TF (1 min / 2 min / 5 min) and your choice is remembered in localStorage. Backend stores per-TF candles separately, so every layer stays internally consistent.

💰

AWS Cost Dashboard

Click the green $ Cost button to see a live AWS spend snapshot: month-to-date spend with vs-last-month delta, projected month-end total, budget limit with utilization bar & alert threshold marker, 7-day daily average, top services breakdown, and severity-tagged budget alerts. Powered by AWS Cost Explorer + Budgets APIs via boto3, with a 1-hour in-process cache to keep CE billing minimal ($0.01/req).

Architecture

How it works

Five focused layers, each doing one job well.

1

Data Fetch

yfinance pulls 5-day / 5-min OHLCV bars every 1 minute for every tracked ticker.

2

Indicators

Python computes EMA, RSI-14, RVOL, VWAP, trend, ORB, session levels, S/R, volume profile, EMA spread, and 9-signal confluence score in-process.

3

AI + Composite Alerts

33-rule engine fires UP/DOWN + confidence. 5 composite detectors check for POWER_TREND, STRUCTURE_BREAK, VWAP_RESET, EXHAUSTION_REVERSAL, and POC_MAGNET patterns.

4

Broadcast

FastAPI WebSocket pushes price, indicator delta, and prediction to authenticated clients instantly.

5

Visualise

React + Zustand merges the stream into local state. Charts and panels update live, no polling.

Composite Alert Patterns

High-conviction setups, explained

Five tiered composite detectors fire only when multiple indicator conditions align simultaneously — no noise, just high-probability setups.

⇕ T1 · POWER_TREND

Power Trend

Full EMA stack aligned (9>21>50 or 9<21<50), price on the correct side of VWAP, and daily trend confirming direction. The highest-conviction setup in the system.

⇕ T1 · STRUCTURE_BREAK

Structure Break

Price breaks through a key support or resistance level with volume confirmation. Signals a potential trend continuation or acceleration after the break.

⇕ T2 · VWAP_RESET

VWAP Reset

Price reclaims or loses VWAP with EMA structure confirming direction. Institutions repositioning around fair value — typically kicks off a directional move.

⇕ T2 · EXHAUSTION_REVERSAL

Exhaustion Reversal

RSI extreme (overbought / oversold) with price near a swing high/low and volume drying up. Momentum is exhausted — fade the move and expect a reversal.

⇕ T3 · POC_MAGNET

POC Magnet

Price is trading away from the session Point of Control (highest-volume node). The POC acts as a gravitational anchor — expect price to revert toward it over the next few bars.

↑↓ 33-RULE AI ENGINE

Per-bar Predictions

Every 5-minute bar also runs the full 33-rule weighted evaluator: EMA stack, VWAP position & motion, RSI, momentum, candle structure, ORB, S/R, and confluence — scoring an UP/DOWN with confidence.
