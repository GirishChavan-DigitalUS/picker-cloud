Double-counting EMA (rules #1–#4)
Rules #1 and #3 fire simultaneously in every uptrend — EMA score inflated by 2.7 (1.5 + 1.2) vs the intended 1.2. This is the biggest scoring distortion in the library. Fix: mutual exclusion.
Confluence score double-counts its own sub-rules
Rules #32/#33 (confluence) fire based on bull_score ≥ 6. But #32 itself has weight 1.8 — which feeds back into future score calculations if not isolated. Confirm that confluence rules do NOT count themselves in the score that triggered them. This is a circular dependency risk.
No market regime filter
All 33 rules treat trending and ranging markets identically. In a range-bound choppy day (VIX spike, pre-Fed announcement), trend signals (#1–#6) become false-signal generators. Add a regime_choppy flag based on ADX < 20 — when true, suppress all trend and momentum signals and only score mean-reversion rules.
No options-specific context (IV / earnings)
For options trading, IV rank and earnings proximity change every signal's risk profile. A 1.8-confidence bull signal on a stock with IV rank 90 and earnings in 2 days is a completely different risk than the same signal on a normal day. At minimum, flag when IV rank > 80 or earnings within 3 days and suppress or warn on directional signals.
No signal staleness / invalidation logic
Once a signal fires, there's no rule that invalidates it. Example: vwap_reclaim_bullish fires, then 2 candles later price drops back below VWAP — the bullish signal is now wrong but still in the score. Add an invalidation check: any signal that is contradicted by a later opposing signal of equal or higher weight should be marked INVALIDATED and removed from the active score.
