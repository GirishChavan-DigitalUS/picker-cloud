1. Overview & Getting Started

Picker is an AI-powered intraday trading dashboard for NYSE equities. It combines real-time technical indicators, rule-based signal detection, optional machine learning, and multi-timeframe confluence analysis to identify high-probability setups during market hours.

Default Ticker Universe

SPY QQQ SPX AAPL GOOGL NVDA TSLA AMZN MSFT PLTR META AMD

You can add or remove tickers on-the-fly from the dashboard.

Market Sessions (US Eastern)

SessionHours (ET)Dashboard Behavior
Pre-Market04:00 – 09:30Historical view, countdown to open
Regular (RTH)09:30 – 16:00Full live dashboard, real-time signals
After-Hours16:00 – 20:00Historical view, last session data
ClosedWeekends & holidaysOff-hours gate with countdown

Timeframes

Picker runs two intraday timeframes. Use the Timeframe Selector in the header to switch:

  • 2m (Primary) — Updates every 2 minutes. Captures fast momentum shifts and scalping setups.
  • 5m (Secondary) — Updates every 5 minutes. Better for swing entries and smoother signals.
Tip: Start with 2m for active trading. Switch to 5m when you want fewer, more deliberate signals with less noise.

Each card in the grid shows:

  • Ticker & price — Color-coded by EMA state (green = bullish, red = bearish)

4. Indicators Reference

EMA — Exponential Moving Averages

Periods: 9, 21, 50 (TradingView formula). Displayed as blue (EMA9) and orange (EMA21) lines on the chart.

ConditionStateMeaning
EMA9 > EMA21BULLISHShort-term momentum is above medium-term
EMA9 < EMA21BEARISHShort-term momentum has rolled over
9 > 21 > 50FULL STACK ↑Strong bullish alignment across all periods
9 < 21 < 50FULL STACK ↓Strong bearish alignment across all periods

EMA Spread % = (EMA9 – EMA21) / EMA21 × 100. Positive = bullish separation; negative = bearish compression.

RSI-14 — Relative Strength Index

14-period RSI using Wilder's smoothing method.

ZoneReadingInterpretation
Overbought> 70Stretched upside; mean reversion risk. Suppresses bullish signals.
Neutral30 – 70Normal range; no directional bias from RSI alone.
Oversold< 30Stretched downside; bounce potential. Suppresses bearish signals.

VWAP — Volume-Weighted Average Price

VWAP = Σ(Typical Price × Volume) / Σ(Volume), where Typical Price = (High + Low + Close) / 3. Resets at each session boundary. Displayed as a cyan/teal line on the chart.

ConditionSignalMeaning
Price > VWAPABOVETrading above institutional fair value — buyers control
Price < VWAPBELOWTrading below fair value — sellers control
Moving AWAY from VWAPTrend confirmationStrong momentum pushing price further from fair value
Converging TO VWAPMean reversionPrice returning to equilibrium; setup may be exhausting
Key insight: The most powerful bullish signal is Price above VWAP AND moving AWAY — this means both position and momentum confirm the move. The AI weights this heavily (1.3× in rule scoring).

Volume Profile & POC

POC (Point of Control) is the price level with the highest cumulative volume in the current session, calculated from a 50-bin histogram.

  • Price > POC — Buyers established control above the session's fair-value zone
  • Price < POC — Sellers dominate below the session's heaviest volume node
  • VAL (Value Area Low) — Lower bound of the 70% volume cluster
  • VAH (Value Area High) — Upper bound of the 70% volume cluster

Support & Resistance

Multi-timeframe levels derived from:

  • 5-min swing highs/lows — 3-bar pivot window for 2m TF, 2-bar for 5m TF
  • 1-hour pivot highs/lows — Resampled from 5-min data
  • Daily pivot highs/lows — Multi-day resampled

Levels within 0.2% of each other are merged. Each level has a strength score based on how many timeframes confirm it and how many times price has tested it.

Session Levels

LevelDefinitionSignificance
ORB High / LowFirst 15 minutes (09:30–09:44 ET)Opening Range Breakout levels — a break above/below often sets the day's direction
Premarket High / Low04:00–09:30 ET rangeKey overnight reference; breaks indicate strong directional conviction
Previous Day High / LowYesterday's RTH extremesMajor support/resistance for the current session
Session POCPOC per session (pre/regular/after)Fair-value anchor for that time window

Daily Trend

Derived from resampling 5-min bars to daily candles. Checks the last 3 daily bars:

  • BULL — Higher highs AND higher lows across 3 days
  • BEAR — Lower highs AND lower lows across 3 days
  • NEUTRAL — No consistent pattern

5. AI Prediction System

Pipeline Architecture

Each prediction flows through a multi-stage pipeline:

  1. Evidence Builder — Computes 30+ features from indicators and candles (timeframe-aware)
  2. Rule Engine — 40+ deterministic rules, each with a weight and direction. Rules fire based on indicator states and candle patterns.
  3. ML Blend (optional) — If a Gradient Boosting model is trained on ≥ 500 samples for this timeframe, it provides a secondary opinion.
  4. Output — Final prediction: UP, DOWN, NEUTRAL, or ABSTAIN + confidence (0–1)

Confidence Scoring

Confidence = (bullish_weight_sum – bearish_weight_sum) / total_weight_sum, clamped to 0–1. If confidence falls below 0.40, the prediction becomes ABSTAIN — meaning the model doesn't have enough conviction to call a direction.

Key Rules (High-Weight Examples)

RuleWeightDirectionTrigger
confluence_strong_bull1.8UP6+ indicators aligned bullish
orb_break_bullish1.4UPPrice breaks ORB High on volume
vwap_bull_momentum1.3UPAbove VWAP + moving AWAY
vwap_reclaim_bullish1.3UPPrice crosses above VWAP this bar
ema_stack_bullish1.2UPFull bullish stack (9 > 21 > 50)
daily_trend_bull1.2UPDaily trend is BULL
sr_support_bounce1.2UPNear support, moving up, normal volume
higher_low_formed1.1UPHH + HL confirmed on swing structure
ema_bullish1.0UPEMA9 > EMA21
price_above_poc1.0UPPrice above Point of Control

Bearish rules are the inverse of each bullish rule with equal weights.

Suppression Rules

These reduce confidence or prevent signals when conditions are ambiguous:

RuleEffectTrigger
ema_cross_imminent×0.7 confidenceEMA9 & EMA21 gap < 0.08% — cross about to flip
rsi_overboughtSuppresses bullishRSI > 70 — upside stretched
rsi_oversoldSuppresses bearishRSI < 30 — downside stretched
volume_dry_up×0.6 confidenceVolume < 0.5× average — no conviction

ML Blend Logic

When the ML model is active for a timeframe:

  • Agreement (rule & ML same direction): Confidence = 0.7 × rule + 0.3 × ML → boosted
  • ML Override (rule abstained, ML ≥ 0.40): ML prediction used, tagged ml_override
  • Disagreement: Rule engine result takes precedence

Outcome Tracking

Every prediction is tracked. After the prediction horizon (10 min for 2m TF, 30 min for 5m TF), the system checks whether price moved in the predicted direction by ≥ 0.1%. Outcomes feed back into ML retraining for continuous improvement.

7. Composite Alerts (High-Conviction Setups)

Composite alerts fire when 3+ indicators align with sufficient AI confidence. They are ranked by tier:

Tier 1 — ACT (Confidence ≥ 0.75)

These are the highest-conviction setups. Consider taking immediate action.

AlertSetup
POWER_TREND_BULLDaily BULL + EMA bullish + Price above VWAP + VWAP moving AWAY. Full macro + micro alignment.
POWER_TREND_BEARInverse of Power Trend Bull. All indicators bearish + macro headwind.
STRUCTURE_BREAK_UPKey level (ORB/PDH/Swing High) broken on RVOL > 1.5× + EMA bullish. Breakout confirmed by volume.
STRUCTURE_BREAK_DOWNKey support broken on high volume. Breakdown confirmed.
VWAP_RESET_BULLPrice converged to VWAP from above, then reclaimed on volume + EMA bullish. Clean re-entry.
VWAP_RESET_BEARInverse — price rallied to VWAP from below, then rejected.
EXHAUSTION_REVERSAL_BULL4+ consecutive red bars, then strong green close on volume. Selling exhaustion detected.
EXHAUSTION_REVERSAL_BEAR4+ consecutive green bars, then strong red close. Buying exhaustion.
POC_MAGNETPrice pulled to POC, held, then moved decisively away on volume.

Tier 2 — WATCH (Confidence ≥ 0.65)

Monitor closely. May need additional confirmation before acting.

AlertSetup
HIGH_CONF_BULLAI prediction UP with ≥ 0.75 confidence + EMA bullish. Single-indicator confirmation.
HIGH_CONF_BEARAI prediction DOWN with ≥ 0.75 confidence + EMA bearish.

Tier 3 — CONTEXT (Informational)

Background signals for awareness. Consolidations, buildups, and lower-confidence readings.

Suppression & Conflict

  • Conflict Warning: If bullish AND bearish alerts fire on the same bar, both are downgraded to Tier 3 with a ⊘ WARNING tag.
  • Prior Rejection: If the prior bar had a strong opposite signal, new signals are suppressed to prevent whipsaws.

8. Pattern Engine & Traffic Lights

The pattern engine runs in real time on each ticker, analyzing the last 20 signal bars plus candle structure to identify the current market pattern. Each pattern produces a traffic light, a label, and an action recommendation.

Patterns (Priority Order)

PatternTraffic LightConditionAction
BREAKOUT 🟢 / 🔴 ≥1 COILING bar in last 5 + current GREEN/RED + confidence ≥ 0.65 Trade the breakout. Momentum confirmed.
FAKEOUT 🟠 Previous COILING + signal flipped sharply Do not chase. Likely a trap. Wait for re-entry.
REJECTION 🟠 Strong prior bar (conf ≥ 0.70), current flipped Do not chase. Potential trap or range boundary.
REVERSAL 🟢 / 🔴 4+ consecutive same-color bars, then flip with ≥ 0.65 confidence Consider entry on confirmation. Trend may be shifting.
BOUNCE 🟡 Last 3 bars: RED → YELLOW → GREEN (or inverse) Watch for follow-through. May be a dead-cat bounce.
CONTINUATION 🟢 / 🔴 ≥3 consecutive same-color bars in matching structure (UPTREND/DOWNTREND) Trade with the trend. Momentum favors the direction.
BUILDUP 🟡 ≥2 COILING bars in last 5 + current not GREEN/RED Energy building. Prepare for a breakout move.
CHOP ZONE ⚫ ≥3 YELLOW bars in last 5 OR ≥3 CHOPPY structure bars Stay flat. No edge. Wait for clean directional setup.

Signal Chip Colors

BUY — Bullish signal SELL — Bearish signal CAUT — Cautious / mixed STAY — Abstain (low confidence)

Signal Age

Minutes since the last color transition — visible on each chip:

  • 0–5 min: Fresh signal, just flipped
  • 5–15 min: Established, likely holding
  • 15+ min: Aging signal, watch for reversal

9. Detail View & Analysis Panels

Click any ticker card to open its detail view (right panel on desktop, full-screen on mobile).

30-Minute Timeline

At the top of the detail view, a visual bar chart shows 30 minutes of trend + structure data. Each column represents one bar with two layers:

  • Trend row (top): Signal color based on AI prediction
  • Structure row (bottom): Candle structure color (green/red/gold/grey)
  • When both rows agree, the setup has maximum conviction

Candlestick Chart

Interactive chart with overlays (see Chart Controls).

Market Read Panel

Displays the current AI prediction, confidence, and the complete list of triggered rules with explanations.

Tape Read / Candle Context Card

Shows structure type, detected candlestick patterns, volume character, pace, close position, HOD/LOD, ATR-14, and the optional LLM-generated senior trader narrative.

Prediction Card

Compact view: direction arrow, confidence %, and top 3 rules.

10. Confluence Analysis

The Confluence Panel merges volume profile levels (POC, VAL, VAH) with multi-timeframe pivot analysis across daily, weekly, and monthly horizons.

Fusion Score (0–1)

Measures how aligned the current setup is across multiple timeframes:

  • ≥ 0.75: Strong confluence — setup validates across timeframes. Highest conviction.
  • 0.50 – 0.74: Moderate confluence — some alignment, worth monitoring.
  • < 0.50: Weak confluence — timeframes disagree. Lower conviction.

Confluence Bias

BULL — Multi-TF bullish alignment BEAR — Multi-TF bearish alignment MIXED — Timeframes conflict

11. Tape Read & Senior Trader Narrative

The optional Senior Trader Read section uses an LLM to synthesize the last 30 minutes of candle data with the current AI evaluation into a 3-line narrative:

  1. Setup Label — ≤ 5 words summarizing the setup (e.g., "Bull Flag Pullback")
  2. Tape vs. Structure — Reconciles any conflict between tape reading and indicator signals, with specific price levels
  3. Next Probable Move — Predicted direction + the confirm/invalidate levels to watch
Note: The Senior Trader Read requires an LLM provider to be configured (Gemini, OpenAI, Anthropic, or Ollama). If LLM is disabled, this section shows a "Generate" button that is inactive.

13. Alerts Panel

The alerts sidebar (desktop: right edge; mobile: bell icon modal) displays composite alerts in reverse chronological order.

Alert Card Anatomy

  • Direction arrow: ▲ UP, ▼ DOWN, or ⚠ WARNING
  • Signal type: e.g., POWER_TREND_BULL, STRUCTURE_BREAK_DOWN
  • Tier badge: ACT / WATCH / CONTEXT
  • Ticker: Bold, right-aligned
  • Time: HH:MM:SS when the alert fired
  • Confidence bar: Color-coded (green ≥ 75%, yellow ≥ 65%, red < 65%)
  • Component chips: Which indicators confirmed (EMA State, VWAP Motion, Daily Trend, etc.)

Click any alert to jump to that ticker's detail view.

14. Mobile & PWA Support

Install as App

Picker is a Progressive Web App (PWA). On iOS Safari, tap Share → "Add to Home Screen". On Android Chrome, tap the install banner or Menu → "Install app". This gives you a native-app experience with offline caching and push notifications.

Mobile-Specific Features

  • Single-column layout — Cards stack vertically for easy scrolling
  • Full-screen detail view — Tap card → opens as modal with back button
  • Bell icon + badge — Shows unseen alert count; tap to open alerts modal
  • Touch gestures — Pinch-zoom and swipe on charts
  • Subscribe to alerts — Tap the bell to enable push notifications for Tier 1 & 2 alerts

15. Push Notifications

When subscribed, you receive push notifications for Tier 1 and Tier 2 composite alerts, even when the app is minimized or your phone is locked.

Notification Format

Picker ▲ AAPL @ $194.53 — Power Trend [2m]
Tier 1 · 78% confidence

Actions

  • Tap notification — Opens Picker and navigates directly to that ticker's detail view
  • Dismiss — Alert is still visible in the alerts panel
  • Unsubscribe — Use the bell icon in the header to disable push
Cross-TF dedup: If the same alert fires on both 2m and 5m within 5 minutes, you only receive one push notification.

16. Market Hours & Off-Hours Mode

During Regular Hours (09:30–16:00 ET)

  • Full live dashboard with real-time WebSocket updates
  • Candles close every 2m or 5m (based on selected TF)
  • Predictions and alerts fire continuously

Outside Market Hours

  • Gate panel replaces the live dashboard
  • Ticker list with last-known prices (still editable — add/remove tickers)
  • Countdown timer: "Market opens in X hours Y minutes" (updates per second)
  • Clicking a ticker shows historical detail view with previous session data
  • WebSocket disabled; no real-time updates

NYSE Holidays

Picker recognizes all NYSE holidays (full closures and early closes at 1:00 PM ET) through 2028. On holidays, the dashboard shows a holiday banner with the holiday name and the next trading day.
